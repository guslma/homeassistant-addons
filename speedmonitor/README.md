_SpeedMonitor tracks your internet speed and outages over time._

## About

📊 Statistics for download, upload, ping, stability and speed by hour of the day

🔌 Records every outage with its start, end and duration, and tells whether it came from the provider or from your home network

📄 Compares the measured speed with your contracted plan month by month and creates a report you can print or save as a PDF

🔔 Sends alerts through Telegram, ntfy, Discord, Gotify, Pushover or a webhook when the internet comes back after an outage or the speed falls below the plan

🧩 Customizable dashboard: reorder, resize and hide the cards

⏰ Scheduled tests with Ookla, LibreSpeed or Cloudflare servers

🔥 Prometheus and Grafana metrics

## Data

The database and settings are kept in the add-on configuration folder, so they are included in Home Assistant backups.
